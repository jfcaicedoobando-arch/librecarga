# Decisiones técnicas

- Opportunity external seller is read-only and resolved by `crm_oportunidad_vendedor_externo` from the company's broker (explicit company link or originating lead), with existing opportunity reader permissions and active tenant checks. Why: show broker attribution without duplicating names or changing KAM assignment or commissions.

- Demo lead CSV export uses the existing formula sanitizer before CSV quoting in `demoLeadsCsv`. Why: untrusted lead fields must open as text, not spreadsheet instructions, without changing stored data or enabling the demo.

- Notas de crédito por embarque: cada renglón de NC copiado de la factura guarda `concepto_factura_id`; `_venta_facturada_por_embarque` resta esa base al embarque del concepto y sólo prorratea renglones sin linaje (manuales o históricos), y el trigger `_nc_validar_linaje_conceptos` rechaza ids ajenos o créditos mayores al subtotal del concepto. Why: atribución exacta sin inventar linaje histórico ni tocar XML/PDF.
- Seguros de embarque: una póliza puede ligarse opcionalmente a la factura de proveedor que documenta su prima (`seguros_embarque.proveedor_factura_id`); el trigger `_seguro_validar_factura_proveedor` exige misma org, factura vigente del embarque (también al restaurar), un índice único impide dos pólizas activas con la misma factura, y `pnl_financiero_embarque` omite la prima ligada porque ya cuenta la factura. Why: no contar dos veces el mismo gasto sin inferir vínculos ni tocar históricos.

- Proveedores provisionales (`estado_alta`): alta vía `crear_agente_provisional`, aprobación sólo contador/admin vía `aprobar_proveedor_provisional`; triggers bloquean facturas/pagos. Why: tarifas sin esperar a Contabilidad, sin pagar a no verificados.

- Replay de provisionales: Drizzle0013/190000 son historia literal;190100 cierra ejecución directa de los dos triggers y190200 corrige sólo los cuatro appends de faltantes, reafirmando la ACL original del RPC sin ampliar roles. La release13.824.46 conserva UI199 e historial35–44 sin crear45; su regresión funcional se registra como guard. Why: separar reconstrucción histórica, corrección forward, pruebas de catálogo y autorización de aplicación remota, sin ocultar drift ni reescribir historia.

- Catálogos globales: anon no tiene privilegios sobre planes/navieras/puertos/tipos_contenedor/tipos_cambio_dof; authenticated conserva CRUD RLS sólo en los primeros cuatro y SELECT en DOF. Planes comunes filtran activo IS TRUE; super_admin conserva inactivos y RPC manual DOF. Why: quitar privilegios fuera de RLS sin romper la administración existente ni la lectura global operativa. Aplicación remota exige envelope revisado y aprobación específica.

- Análisis financiero: DSO/DPO usan cartera promedio y actividad propia a crédito neta documentada del mismo mes/moneda; runway exige tres meses calendario completos cerrados de consumo operativo y caja conciliada con restricciones al corte explícito aplicable, no anterior al fin observado ni futuro respecto de la fecha de negocio. Why: los cinco criterios aprobados el 2026-10-13 fijan políticas, no certifican datos; lectores actuales y flags/IDs del cliente mantienen N/D sin fallback de EERR o próximos 30 días.

- Demo deshabilitada (13.824.84): UI sin formulario/leads/login, Edge sin proveedor y 503 previo a Auth/SQL, y cuatro rutas SQL bloqueadas antes de DML. No revoca sesiones ni cambia identidades; no habilitar mediante flags/request ni restaurar el seed destructivo. Why: cerrar la release mientras se revisan procedencia y regeneración segura.

- Subdirectorios (`src/features/crm`, `cotizacion`, `proformas`, `cxp`) tienen su propio `AGENTS.md`: léelo antes de cambiar archivos ahí.
- Handover detail is read-only and loads the existing handover row with quotation and active-organization filters; reuse quotation operational data, never generate handovers from the UI. Why: expose the existing handover without changing notification recipients, assignment, or tenant policies.
- Quotation-to-shipment draft creation only persists the shipment link; `sync_cotizacion_embarque_link` also listens to shipment status and promotes accepted quotations only for operational states, excluding draft, quotation and cancelled. Why: confirmation, not automatic draft creation, starts operations while preserving cancellation housekeeping.

- Shipment draft margin approval uses a server-calculated monetary snapshot and transactional notifications; the UI keeps the previous save guard until the candidate approval RPC is deployed, while operational shipment edits retain their existing commercial guard. Why: preparation must not enable unapproved operations on the active database or invalidate approval for provider hydration.
- Commercial minimum margin stays in `useMargenMinimo` / `comercial/margen_minimo_utilidad_pct` and is changed only by `set_margen_minimo_utilidad`. Why: keep one configurable threshold shared with quotations.
- Low-margin labels use a tenant-scoped batched RPC; operational monetary changes record the authenticated author and final snapshot transactionally in the candidate approval/audit records. Why: identify exceptions without trusting browser identity, guessing historical approvals, or querying per row; activation remains separately authorized.
- Internal supplier resolution, blocking-payment counts, fiscal receiver validation and container delay calculations are owner/service-only; audit snapshots are API read-only and captured through the existing server-derived RPC. Why: prevent direct cross-tenant lookups and client-authored audit scores without changing business calculations or approved workflows.

- Edge sin `deno.json` propio: todo `npm:` usado en `supabase/functions/_shared` debe existir en `package.json` (devDependency con la misma versión exacta). Why: el chequeo de Deno resuelve contra `node_modules` y falla si el paquete falta.
- Cambios antiguos no aplicados: `supabase/migrations/*` es historia inmutable; lo que nunca llegó a producción se aplica como migración Drizzle nueva partiendo de la definición viva (0042–0048), nunca reaplicando el archivo viejo. Why: los archivos de 2026-07/09 quedaron desactualizados y revertirían correcciones posteriores.
- Keep selected tariff currency in quotation form state separately from CRM currency and use it in step 3 and final sale payloads; never change step-1 CRM linkage or silently add unlike currencies. Why: selected-option denomination without weakening existing linkage and conversion guards.
- Portal de Brokers: rol `broker` confinado a `/broker`; identidad y % por organización en `crm_brokers` (alta sólo por la acción `invite-broker` de user-management, sin membresía interna) y todo el portal (lectura, búsqueda discreta, peticiones `crm_broker_solicitudes` y alta atómica `broker_alta_prospecto` con KAM por turno bajo advisory lock) sólo por RPC `broker_*`, nunca por tablas CRM. Why: vendedores externos sin acceso a datos internos.
- Broker commissions retain the service table and payment proration; `_comision_kam_broker` keeps the internal accrual anchor but zeros unliquidated KAM amounts for broker-owned companies, without changing `calcular_comision_pago`, assignment or non-broker commissions. Why: the broker mirror depends on that anchor; suppressing its insertion would suppress the broker too.
- Membership and payment-ledger readers use SECURITY INVOKER; any reviewed helper privilege change must update only its pinned Pricing catalog entry atomically, retaining all other fingerprints and guards. Why: enforce existing row access without reintroducing Pricing permission drift.
- Payment-detail reads use SECURITY INVOKER; shipment delay calculations retain privileged transactional writes but mirror shipment SELECT policies before loading the row, including the active-tenant restriction for superadmins. Why: protect read results without breaking existing delay triggers, tariff calculations or fiscal locks.
- Bank-account locks mirror account SELECT policies while retaining DEFINER execution for invoker payment callers; supplier rejection internals are owner/service-only, and shipment activity scopes every audit branch to its organization. Why: prevent direct bypass and same-folio cross-tenant history without changing authorized writes.
- Selected quotation linking, shipment duplication and proforma creation add organization-membership role checks without changing shared writer helpers; payment removal uses the existing exact financial-role list locally, and quotation version costs mirror current cost-reader permissions. Why: global roles must not override the effective organization role or expose costs through version readers.
